Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026 • Order Status Guidelines
Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions Shipping & Delivery No Return / No Refund Cancellation Policy Grievance Support
Prompt Notification: Because orders are processed and confirmed directly through WhatsApp, customers should request cancellation as soon as possible after placing an order. Once an order is prepared, packed, or dispatched, cancellation is no longer possible.

1 Cancellation Before Processing

A customer may request cancellation by contacting Rachayati Designs through WhatsApp or the available customer-support contact details.

If the order has not yet been processed, custom prepared, or prepared for courier dispatch, Rachayati Designs may be able to cancel the order.

2 Cancellation After Processing

Once an order has been processed, prepared, packed, dispatched with a courier, or otherwise entered into fulfillment, cancellation may not be possible.

3 Refunds Following Cancellation

If a cancellation request is accepted before processing and a refund is applicable, the applicable refund timeline and process will be communicated to the customer.

Where a refund is not applicable under our policies or applicable law, the customer will be clearly informed accordingly.

4 Customized or Special Orders

Orders involving customization, personalization, tailored dimensions, special altar requirements, or products specially prepared for a customer cannot be cancelled once preparation or crafting has commenced.

5 How to Request Cancellation

To request a cancellation, please contact us immediately with your order details and the phone number used when placing the order:
